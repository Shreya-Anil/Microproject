Why rooftop solar fits India

Most of India gets strong sun for much of the year, and a home system turns that into a lower bill.

☀️

Cleaner air

From bill to switch-on in four steps

  1. Check your roof. You need about 10 m2 of shade-free space per kW.
  2. Size the system. The dashboard turns your monthly bill into kW.
  3. Apply for subsidy. Register on the national portal and pick a listed vendor.
  4. Install and net-meter. The electricity board fits a two-way meter.

Common questions

Does solar work in the rainy season?

Yes, at lower output. Monthly generation drops about 15% in monsoon months.

What happens during a power cut?

A grid-tied system switches off for safety. Add a hybrid inverter and battery for backup.

How do I get help here?

Press 💬 at the bottom right and ask the assistant.

Quick tips

Face south

Tilt panels near your latitude, roughly equal to your city’s latitude.

Avoid shade

A single shaded panel can cut a whole string's output.

Keep it clean

Wipe dust every few weeks.
